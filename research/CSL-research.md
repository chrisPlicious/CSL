## 9. Current website audit (cslinteriorsalliedservices.weebly.com)

**Platform:** Weebly, free plan (footer reads "Powered by Weebly"). Uses a classic red theme variant. Site ID 586509862968192261. Running on the same account since 2013.

**Page map:**

| Nav label | URL slug |
|---|---|
| Home | `/` |
| About Us | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-355432.html` |
| Services | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-428874.html` |
| Finished Projects | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom.html` |
| Clients | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-609403.html` |
| Products | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-344785.html` |
| Contact Us | `/httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-194826.html` (contact form: name, email, comment) |

**Problems:**
1. **Everything is a picture.** Home, About, Services, Clients and Products are each a single flyer image, so search engines can't read the copy and screen readers can't either. Every image has the alt text "Picture".
2. **Broken slugs.** Someone pasted the site URL in as page names, which produced `httpscslinteriorsalliedservicesweeblycom-NNNNNN.html`. One indexed URL (`…-344785-660172.html`) now returns 404.
3. **Duplicate meta.** Five pages share the same meta description, and it is cut off mid-sentence ("…drywall installation,"). The keywords field is cut off at "maso". The Products page meta lists products the page doesn't show.
4. **AI-generated look.** The home and about flyers use generic AI or stock renders (a marble lobby, a hard hat), not CSL's own work. The logo is AI-generated, and there are two different logo versions.
5. **Mixed identity.** The header still says the old "Best Value Products and Best Service". The legacy Gmail address appears next to the new domain email. The free Weebly subdomain still carries the old "alliedservices" name.
6. **Thin Products page.** It shows blinds only, even though CSL has a decade of carpet, vinyl, wallpaper and partition supply.
7. **Typos.** "Pain Finishes", "Fix Ceiling" (should be "Fixed"), "Accoustic", "conductors" (should be "contractors"), "constractor" in an old indexed title.
8. **No trust content.** No PCAB license or category, no years-in-business figure, no testimonials, no team or leadership info, no map, no click-to-call or Messenger link.
9. Project galleries have no captions or before/after pairing, and Mandaue City is the only location filter.

## 11. Notes for the redesign

- **Positioning:** a Cebu fit-out and general contractor with 15 years of interiors experience. The clear differentiator is **in-house supply plus install of interior finishes**: blinds, carpet, vinyl, acoustic ceilings, drywall.
- **Strongest proof:** the hospital and healthcare fit-outs (8 healthcare clients, plus San Lucas dialysis and birthing rooms), the universities, the Mactan industrial park (Taiyo Yuden), and IT Park call centers.
- **Domain:** move to `cslinteriors-construction.com.ph`. The domain is already owned and its email works. Redirect the Weebly site to it.
- **Must-ask from the client:** PCAB license number and category, SEC registration year, years in business (2011 means 15 years), team photos and leadership, project years and floor areas, permission to show client logos, testimonials, better project photos, and the original vector logo.
- **Content to rebuild as real HTML text:** services (6 disciplines), projects (11 case studies with scope lists), clients (43 logos grouped by sector), products (full catalog), mission, vision and values.
- **Pick one tagline.** "Building Spaces. Creating Value. Delivering Trust." is the strongest.
